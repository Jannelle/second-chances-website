The Timeline

  1. There was a reality show called First Try that ran for three seasons
  2. Some castmembers who didn't end up married from First Try were invited to come back for a new show called Second Chances
  3. Near the beginning of Second Chances, Jannelle died and production stopped
  4. After the exact amount of time needed to make the storyline work, production started again

When the game starts, we will be in a "flashback" to the time before Jannelle died. The host of the show will do a brief interview of each castmember so that everyone can get to know the characters a bit, then Jannelle will die, and we will jump to the present day.

Btw the timeline/seasons doesn't actually matter that much. I just needed a way to split people up into management group sizes because I was getting confused during writing.

Important Note

Just like in real reality shows, the game is rigged. Some of you will have a harder time than others, and sometimes things will just happen because they make for good TV. I am not your fair and just DM: I am your ruthless reality TV producer who cares most about good ratings. Roll with the punches and go with the flow.

Main Goals

  1. Figure out the murderer and their motive.
  2. Get married (ideally, to your Second Chance).
  3. Have fun.

Anti-Goals

  1. Do not marry the murderer.
  2. Do not let the murderer discover your secret.

Tips

  • If you are stuck, talk to the staff (Jannelle and Monica). I will do my best to provide direction, but if you need me, I'll Be There For You by the Remembrandts. I'll also be observing to make sure the show is going well.
  • No one is expecting serious acting. The characters are designed to be silly, over-the-top, and campy. Lean into it. We'll all have more fun.
  • Talk to people! Not everyone was everywhere all at once, so no one can solve this murder alone. You'll have to consolidate multiple people's information to figure it all out.
  • Protect your secret from the killer, but find the people you can trust. Your Second Chance will want to make sure your secrets match, so sharing it (or hinting at it) might make them more likely to accept your proposal.
  • Alibis matter. If someone can confirm where you were, that should help clear you. Who will be on your side?
  • Not everyone is telling the truth. Whose story is being corroborated? Who is telling the truth?
  • New information will be discovered throughout the night. You don't need to lock in on a killer from the beginning.
